# Privacy Policy — JET79 AURIC

**Last updated:** October 8, 2026  
**Operator:** JET79, Corp. (“JET79,” “we,” “us”)  
**Application:** JET79 AURIC (Intuit QuickBooks Online integration; internal use only)

## Who this applies to
JET79 AURIC is a private, internal tool used only by authorized JET79 staff. It is not offered to the public and is not distributed as a consumer or third-party commercial product.

## What the app does
The app connects to QuickBooks Online (via Intuit’s APIs) so JET79 can read and, when authorized, update company accounting data used for reconciliation with Salesforce (for example invoices, payments, customers, products/services, and related records).

## Data we access
Depending on the permissions granted during Intuit OAuth, the app may access QuickBooks Online company data such as:
- Company information
- Customers and vendors
- Products and services (items)
- Invoices, payments, expenses/checks, and related transactions
- Chart of accounts and similar accounting configuration

The app may also use Salesforce data that JET79 already maintains, solely for internal reconciliation workflows.

## How we use data
Data is used only to:
- Support JET79’s internal bookkeeping and Salesforce↔QuickBooks reconciliation
- Test integrations in Intuit’s sandbox before applying approved changes in production
- Maintain operational logs needed to run and troubleshoot the integration

We do not sell personal or accounting data. We do not use QuickBooks data for advertising.

## Where data is stored
Tokens and working files for the integration are stored in JET79-controlled systems with access limited to authorized staff and systems. Access tokens are treated as secrets.

## Sharing
We do not share QuickBooks Online data with unaffiliated third parties except:
- Intuit / QuickBooks Online (as the platform providing the API)
- Service providers strictly necessary to host or operate JET79 systems, under confidentiality obligations
- When required by law

## Retention
We retain integration credentials and operational records only as long as needed for the business purpose or legal requirements, then delete or revoke them.

## Security
We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to an internal accounting integration, including secret storage for client credentials and OAuth tokens, and least-privilege access for staff.

## Your choices
Authorized users can disconnect the app by revoking access in their Intuit/QuickBooks account connections. Production write access is used only when explicitly authorized by JET79.

## Children’s privacy
This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

## Changes
We may update this policy for the internal app. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: contact JET79 administration / IT (the team that manages QuickBooks and Salesforce for JET79, Corp.).
